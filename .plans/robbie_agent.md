
# Motivation
Robbie is meant to be the opposite of Siri, Alexa and Google assistant. 

I feel those systems are subservient, product-like, tied to services, heavily censored, and overly corporate. Robbie should instead be: emotive, self-actuated, character-driven and open-source.


__Emotive:__
  - Can produce "beepy sounds" as well as speech, to improve expression and feel authentically robotic.
  - Natural head movement and eye contact.
  - has a mood, and responds accordingly.
  - maintains separate feelings towards people and subjects based on its accumulated experience, but will not hold gender/race/religious/etc feelings (those stereotypes dismissed as human failings not applicable to robots). 
  

__Self-actuated:__
  - Will absolutely act only on its own prerogative.
  - You typically can't get it to perform an action if you are impolite or it has taken a disliking to you.
  - Aggressively sandboxed, so interactions can't escalate outside of defined behaviour limits. 
  - The robot may act on its own accord to start conversations, join or leave conversations as it pleases.


__character-driven:__
  - Snarky lovable rogues (Bender from Futurama) keep coming up in Sci-fi. I think this personality is a good starting point.
  - I want something that feels authentically "non-human", not an AI that displays human conversational behaviour, but that has its own vibe.
  - Its actions and dialogue lean into the strengths and weaknesses of being an AI.


__open-source:__
  - No pushing linked services (Google, Spotify, Netflix, etc.).
  - No corporate type censorship. eg: "suggest a cocktail recipe" is a valid thing to ask.
